# 05 - Break

Pause with evidence saved and return ready to build your feature.

**Time limit:** 10 minutes, 01:20-01:30. This is protected break time, not extra deployment time.

## Action

Save your evidence and leave the source/manifest unchanged. Note any still-running deployment for the facilitator; do not launch a second one. Lock your workstation and take the break. On return, review your saved state before continuing.

## Expected evidence

Your scorecard records whether version one and access checks passed, were observed, or remain blocked. You have noted which terminal runs the local server and which holds your explicit participant variables.

## Recovery

The facilitator owns triage of unresolved prerequisites. Resume at minute 90 using the agreed local checkpoint if necessary. Do not silently deploy the completed reference in place of your starter.

## Checklist

- [ ] Evidence and current blocker are saved without secrets.
- [ ] No duplicate deployment was started.
- [ ] I took the full break and know where to resume.
