# 10 - Compare your actual stack

Evaluate both the platform responsibilities you exercised and how well you directed and checked CoCo's work.

**Time limit:** 10 minutes, 02:40-02:50. Use 4 minutes for evidence, 4 for responsibility/delegation review, and 2 for a next experiment.

## Action

Bring actual deployment times, approvals, diffs, tests and blocked checks to the [scorecard](../scorecard.md).

[Prompt CoCo: Review our evidence, responsibilities and delegation](../coco-prompts/review-scorecard.md)

## Your decision

Correct CoCo's proposed notes against the evidence. Label participant-completed, checkpoint-assisted, facilitator-observed and blocked work. Compare four short lists with your actual stack: what you configured, what Snowflake managed in this tested path, what the administrator prepared, and what your team still owns. Include infrastructure you already reuse; there is no predetermined systems-avoided count.

Also record the context you supplied, boundaries you set, a claim you challenged, evidence you inspected and a decision that remained human. Name two candidate internal use cases with one constraint each, then choose one next experiment. "Not a fit" or "needs more testing" is a valid conclusion.

## Expected evidence

A reviewed scorecard with measured results, remaining responsibilities, delegation lessons, at least one limitation, and an experiment owner/success condition. This owner's-rights workshop is not proof of per-user authorization or production readiness. Use the [FAQ](12-honest-faq.md) for a scoped comparison, not a universal AWS mapping.

## Recovery

Missing proof stays blocked with a named evidence requirement. At minute 167, prioritize deployment result, identity caveat, cost owner, one delegation lesson and next experiment. Finish by minute 170 to protect cleanup.

## Checklist

- [ ] Outcomes and fallback use match recorded evidence.
- [ ] Real responsibilities compared without a fixed service count.
- [ ] Context, boundaries, approvals and evidence review assessed.
- [ ] Two candidate use cases have constraints; next experiment has an owner and success condition.
