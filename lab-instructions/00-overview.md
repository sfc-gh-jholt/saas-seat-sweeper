# 00 - Business workflow and outcome

Meet the app you'll build, then get ready to work in your own account.

**Time limit:** 10 minutes, 00:00-00:10. Watch the demo, get a short briefing, and check that you're ready to start.

## Action

Watch the facilitator open SaaS Seat Sweeper, find an inactive license, read its contract, and flag it for review. This is the feature you'll add later. Flagging asks someone to review a seat; it does not cancel the license or save money automatically.

Open the cloned repository root in CoCo. You'll work in `starter-app/`; `reference-app/` is the completed example and is not your deployment target.

Use this prompt for a short introduction. It should read just two documents, not inspect the application.

[Prompt CoCo: Get a quick lab briefing](../coco-prompts/workshop-orient.md)

## Your decision

You work individually. Let CoCo help with each exercise, but check its proposed changes before approving them. When it needs account access, make sure it names your assigned workshop account, not another saved connection.

The pattern is simple: **inspect, propose, approve, act, verify**. You'll practice it as you go; you don't need to plan the entire session now.

## Expected evidence

Before continuing, check that:

- You have the repository open and know where `starter-app/` is.
- The facilitator has confirmed account setup and given you the connection to use.
- Your own app has not been deployed yet. You'll deploy it twice during the lab.

Write your name and assigned connection in the [scorecard](../scorecard.md). The [introduction](../introduction.md) has the full agenda. Aim to have your first endpoint by minute 65.

## Recovery

If CoCo starts exploring the whole repository, stop it and use the short prompt above. You can also read the introduction yourself and continue.

If account setup is incomplete, ask the facilitator for help. Don't switch to a different account or change authentication/network settings to get past an error. The facilitator follows [administrator preflight](../admin-preflight.md); you don't need to repeat it here.

## Checklist

- [ ] I have my own account and the right connection.
- [ ] I opened the repository root and found the starter.
- [ ] I understand what flagging a license does.
