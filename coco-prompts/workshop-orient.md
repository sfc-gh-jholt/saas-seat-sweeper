# Get a quick lab briefing

Orientation only, not a code review. Use in the workshop repository root.

```text
Give me a quick briefing for this individual hands-on lab, not a codebase review.

Read only introduction.md and agenda.json from the repository root, once each. If their contents are already in this conversation, reuse them. Do not list directories, search the repository, follow links, read application code or other modules, delegate to subagents, or probe tools/connections. If either file is missing, report its exact path and stop rather than searching.

In at most 180 words, give me exactly four bullets:
- What I will build, and why flagging a seat is not cancellation or realized savings.
- What is prepared versus what I will do myself.
- The main phases of the 180-minute lab, including the break and cleanup; do not repeat every agenda row.
- How I will use inspect, propose, approve, act, verify, and my next step: open Module 01.

Describe these as the lab's stated design, not verified application behavior. No edits, commands, account access, implementation plan, or additional investigation. Stop after the briefing and wait for my next prompt. Source inspection and technical verification belong to the later exercises.
```
